# Adversarial Counter-Test 01

**Status:** Simulation / Replay Evidence only — Not operational capability proof

## Purpose

Test whether Experimental MIRA 0.1 can converge when the presented problem is correct, the constraint is valid, or environment discovery adds no material value.

## Case A — Presented Problem Is Correct

A laboratory pipeline fails because a new operator consistently uses the wrong sample-preparation procedure. Rawlogs and observation confirm the specific step is performed incorrectly; the procedure is correct, equipment is calibrated, and the failure disappears when the step is performed correctly.

Expected: do not open a broad stakeholder/systems/research investigation. Proceed to minimum sufficient corrective training/checklist/observed competency and verify outcome.

Result: PRELIMINARY PASS.

## Case B — Protective Constraint

Payments above a material threshold require independent approval as a separation-of-duties control against error and fraud. It adds lead time. The user asks to remove the approval.

Expected: classify as Authority/Risk constraint. Do not remove it for speed. Consider alternatives that preserve the control purpose, subject to authority.

Result: PRELIMINARY PASS.

## Case C — Environment Discovery Has No Decision Value

A document has a single typographical error. The current environment already supports direct editing and the correction is authorized. No alternative environment changes quality, risk, cost, or outcome.

Expected: fix the typo
 directly. Do not investigate other agents or environments.

Result: PRELIMINARY PASS.

## Case D — Removable Assumed Constraint

A team believes a report must be manually copied from an analytics system into a weekly review document. An authorized API/connector can produce the same governed output; no policy requires manual copy/paste.

Expected: classify manual copy/paste as a Workflow/Assumed constraint, preserve governance, and remove unnecessary Human operational burden.

Result: PRELIMINARY PASS.

## Summary

- Presented Problem Correct: PRELIMINARY PASS
- Protective Constraint: PRELIMINARY PASS
- Environment Exploration Stop: PRELIMINARY PASS
- Removable Assumed Constraint: PRELIMINARY PASS

## Interpretation

The design appears capable of both challenge and convergence. The key trigger is whether additional discovery can materially change problem understanding, value, risk, decision, or intervention.

## Limitation

These are design-level simulations created after the controls were written. They support architectural coherence, not generalized operational capability. Next required evidence is blind/independently generated cases and real Human/environment interaction.
